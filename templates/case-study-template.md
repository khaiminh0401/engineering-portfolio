# Case study title

[Portfolio](../README.md)

**Status:** Draft / evidence-backed retrospective. Choose one and record the source/date before publication.

**Evidence boundary:** List what is candidate-confirmed, source-supported, reconstructed, proposed or future work. Record personal ownership and confidentiality limits.

## 1. Context

Explain the system, users and your responsibility. Cite approved evidence. Separate professional production work from personal exploration.

## 2. Problem Statement

State the observable problem and why it mattered. Mark unknowns rather than inventing an incident or impact.

## 3. Technical Constraints

List correctness, performance, operational, compatibility and delivery constraints that actually applied.

## 4. Investigation and Reasoning

Record hypotheses, observations and what ruled alternatives in or out. Separate a proposed investigation from a completed one.

## 5. Architecture / Design Options

Compare credible options against the constraints. State if the options are retrospective or proposed.

## 6. Selected Approach

Explain the decision and why it fit. If not yet confirmed or implemented, label it Proposed.

## 7. Implementation Details

Link safe source at an immutable commit, tests and configuration. Use a short synthetic example only when it adds meaning and label it Reconstructed example.

## 8. Trade-offs

Explain costs, failure modes, operational burden and the conditions for revisiting the choice.

## 9. Validation and Evaluation

Separate observed results from proposed methodology. Give workload/environment/date, baseline and candidate artifacts, acceptance criteria and limitations. Unknown results stay unknown.

## 10. Lessons Learned

State what evidence changed your understanding and what remains unresolved. Label general recommendations separately from personal lessons.

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] Personal ownership and production/personal scope confirmed.
- [ ] Every implementation claim has source or explicit candidate confirmation.
- [ ] Results have dated artifacts; configuration thresholds are not reported as measurements.
- [ ] Proposed work is visibly labeled; no invented incident or outcome.
- [ ] No confidential source, customer information, credentials or infrastructure detail.
- [ ] Links work, terminology is consistent and the resume claim maps back to this study.
